## Summary

- **CPA:** Core financials, close, audit support, and major US GAAP/IFRS topics (leases, revenue, EPS, FX, tax, impairment, segments, M&A, equity method, stock comp, consolidation, statutory, bank rec). No SEC/XBRL or full subledger.
- **CFA:** Core equity valuation (DCF, comps, precedent, LBO), portfolio management, and corporate finance/analysis. No fixed income or full derivatives curriculum.

For how the reporting standard is chosen by country and entity type (e.g. Canada: IFRS vs ASPE), see [GAAP_AND_JURISDICTION.md](GAAP_AND_JURISDICTION.md).
